Recommended experiments & hypotheses

  1. Dead waiting time in bot battles pacing confidence low
    Players who finish before the bot leave during the non-interactive wait, lowering battle completion and replay.
    Intervention: When the human has finished, resolve the bot's (already simulated) result immediately and show the result screen. Outcome probabilities are unchanged.
    F-CODE-pacing.bot_wait.easy F-CODE-pacing.bot_wait.medium F-CODE-pacing.bot_wait.hard F-CODE-pacing.matchmaking F-CODE-battle-params
  2. Ambiguous answer format in numeric questions question_quality confidence low
    Players who get a "wrong" verdict on a correct-but-differently-formatted answer feel cheated and abandon the game or stop trusting the bank.
    Intervention: Show reviewed, clarified wording (explicit unit and rounding) for the flagged questions.
    F-Q-ambiguous-format
  3. Quant Battle is long and its blitz never ends early pacing confidence low
    The match length exceeds what casual visitors budget for, so a share leave before the result screen, especially on mobile.
    Intervention: Test a shorter format (e.g. 60s blitz and a 6-minute tactical cap) once duration data shows how often players run to the limit.
    F-CODE-pacing.quant_battle.easy F-CODE-pacing.quant_battle.hard
  4. IB battles always serve the same three questions question_quality confidence low
    Returning players see no novelty in IB battles and stop replaying them; this depresses repeat play for that segment.
    Intervention: Expand IB pools (at least 15 per tier) before any experiment; meanwhile, hide IB battles or label them as a fixed set.
    F-Q-ib-pool
