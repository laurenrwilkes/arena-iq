Recommended experiments & hypotheses

  1. Dead waiting time in bot battles pacing confidence medium
    Players who finish before the bot leave during the non-interactive wait, lowering battle completion and replay.
    Intervention: When the human has finished, resolve the bot's (already simulated) result immediately and show the result screen. Outcome probabilities are unchanged.
    F-CODE-pacing.bot_wait.easy F-CODE-pacing.bot_wait.medium F-CODE-pacing.bot_wait.hard F-CODE-pacing.matchmaking F-GA-funnel-battle_finish F-CODE-battle-params
  2. Unclear next step after a game pacing confidence medium
    Players finish one game and leave because the next game is not the obvious, one-tap default.
    Intervention: After measuring post_game_action, test a single primary "Play again" CTA, with sign-up moved below for guests.
    F-GAP-next_game_start_rate F-GA-funnel-battle_finish F-GA-funnel-solo_finish F-GA-tenure
  3. Mobile friction in core game loops device confidence medium
    Input-heavy screens (code editor, numeric entry with live check) are harder on touch keyboards, so mobile players abandon more.
    Intervention: Instrument per-question first-input time on mobile; then test a numeric keypad (inputmode="decimal") and hide code battles on small screens.
    F-GA-devicegap-mobile-battle_finish F-GA-devicegap-mobile-practice_view_to_submit F-GA-device-mobile F-GA-device-desktop
  4. Quant Battle is long and its blitz never ends early pacing confidence medium
    The match length exceeds what casual visitors budget for, so a share leave before the result screen, especially on mobile.
    Intervention: Test a shorter format (e.g. 60s blitz and a 6-minute tactical cap) once duration data shows how often players run to the limit.
    F-CODE-pacing.quant_battle.easy F-CODE-pacing.quant_battle.hard F-GA-funnel-battle_finish F-GAP-game_duration_distribution
  5. Ambiguous answer format in numeric questions question_quality confidence low
    Players who get a "wrong" verdict on a correct-but-differently-formatted answer feel cheated and abandon the game or stop trusting the bank.
    Intervention: Show reviewed, clarified wording (explicit unit and rounding) for the flagged questions.
    F-Q-ambiguous-format F-CODE-flags-lost F-GAP-question_solve_rate F-GAP-question_dispute_rate
